02 // Proven Execution at Scale

Selected Case Studies

CASE STUDY 01 • UNITY Scale: ~5,000 Active Global Users

U-AI Workspace (Enterprise AI Operating System at Unity)

THE PROBLEM

Surging demand for GenAI led to fragmented POCs, security risks, and uncontrolled token burn while external tools lacked internal context.

STRATEGY & LEAN MVP

Evaluated the full multi-cloud AI landscape (AWS Q & Bedrock, GCP Vertex & AI Studio, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, LiteLLM) and focused internal R&D strictly on indexing proprietary data moats across corporate repositories (Slack, Confluence, Drive, Zendesk, Gainsight, and core enterprise systems).

SOLUTION & UX

Co-architected a Dual-Experience Platform (zero-friction Slack/Web assistant alongside an "Advanced Lab" for technical power users), dynamic RAG, prompt caching, and semantic routing to lightweight SLMs.

IMPACT: Scaled organically to ~5,000 users, eliminated hallucination retry loops, slashed inference costs, and built a secure corporate intelligence moat.
